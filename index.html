Abstract

Most of what I build sits in one of three rooms: cloud platforms that have to behave under load, AI systems that have to behave under ambiguity, and small finished products that have to behave under a user. I prefer the kind of project where the boundary between "demo" and "shipping" is enforced — where the thing either runs or it doesn't.

This page is the index. Each entry has a number, a year, a stack, and a link back to the source. There is no separate writing section, no blog, no newsletter. The work is the writing.

I claim no breakthrough. I claim a shape for the work and a record of it shipping.
